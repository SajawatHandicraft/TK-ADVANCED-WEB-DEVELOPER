Common questions

Things worth knowing.

Can my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. The layout is designed to adapt to mobile, tablet and desktop screens. Final testing should be done on actual devices too.

Can I host my website for free?

Static websites can be hosted free on platforms such as GitHub Pages, subject to their terms and limits. Custom domains and some additional services may cost extra.

Will my website appear on Google immediately?

No. SEO setup helps search engines understand your pages, but indexing and rankings are not guaranteed or immediate.

How do I request a quote?

Fill in the project form below and continue to WhatsApp. Review the message and send it to submit your enquiry.

Do all advanced features work without a backend?

No. Static HTML can provide the design and client-side interactions. User accounts, persistent databases and payment processing need suitable external services or a backend.
